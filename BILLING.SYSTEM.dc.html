How routing works
1A payment arrives on one of your shortcodes. The system looks up which site profile owns that shortcode.
2The account code or MAC is matched only against clients on that site's router — so identical account codes at different sites never collide.
3The captive portal at that site shows its own paybill or fires STK against its own KopoKopo till.
4Revenue reports and settlement can be split per site without any manual reconciliation.
